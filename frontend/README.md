# InsightUBC

InsightUBC is a data management platform for course and building related information on UBC's Vancouver Campus. 

### Bulk Uploads

InsightUBC accepts course and duilding information directly to the website. Upload a .zip file with either courses or buildings content to the site to process hundreds of courses at once. Uploads are persistent.

### Filtering and Pagination

InsightUBC also offers the ability to filter through course and building data to quickly locate a specific section or room. clients can also filter and limit entries by a specific name, size, average, keyword, or other datapoints. clients can also limit the number of courses or buildings presented to them on a single page.

## Insights

InsightUBC offers three separate ways for a client to visualize their data in the database: an **Averages Graph**, a **Capacity Graph**, and a **Section Assignments** 

### Averages Graph

The averages graph allows clients to graph a course's average over time, and compare those averages to other courses. A client can choose to graph the mean grade (average), mean pass, fail, and audit counts.\
\
This insight is useful for both students and UBC faculty to better showcase current trends in course averages, pass rates, and fail rates for UBC courses. Decision makers at UBC can use the graphed data determine when changes to course content might be neccessary or beneficial. A client can also use this tool to analyze the effects of changes in course content.

### Capacity Graph

The capacity graph allows clients to visually display and compare building capacities. A client can filter buildings by name, address, and minimum total seats. \
\
The capacity graph will allow clients to visually compare building capacities with eachother. A client could use this information to better locate space for future sections or courses at UBC.

### Section Assignments

The section assignments page allows clients to link course sections to rooms and buildings. A client can choose to filter the diagram by course department, code, year, or building name. \
\
The section assignments diagram will allow a client to simulate section scheduling to physical spaces. This helps manage the assignment of future courses to rooms in buildings by providing a visualization of how sections might have been assigned in previous years.
